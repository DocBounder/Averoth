## 🛡️ Dame Serel Thorne
*Medium humanoid (Church Champion), Lawful Neutral*

**Armor Class** 19 (plate, Defender training)  
**Hit Points** 108 (16d10+32)  
**Speed** 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----:|----:|----:|----:|----:|----:|
| 22 (+6) | 16 (+3) | 22 (+6) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Con +9, Str +9, Cha +3  
**Skills** Athletics +9, Insight +6, Intimidation +6  
**Damage Resistances** Radiant  
**Senses** passive Perception 16  
**Languages** Common, Celestial  
**Challenge** 5 (1,950 XP)

---

### Aura of Command
Allies within 20 feet of Serel gain a +1 bonus to AC and saving throws.

### Sacred Stride
Dame Serel ignores difficult terrain created by magical effects.

### Unshakable Duty
Serel is immune to being charmed, frightened, or magically compelled.

---

### Multiattack
Dame Serel makes two longsword attacks.

### Longsword
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 13 (2d8+6) slashing damage.

### Shield Slam (Recharge 4-6)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit:* 13 (2d8+6) bludgeoning damage.  
The target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone and stunned until the end of its next turn.

### Radiant Smite (Bonus Action)
The next weapon attack Serel makes before the end of her turn deals an additional 9 (2d8) radiant damage.

### Anchor Howl (2 Charges)
As a forceful declaration of authority, Dame Serel strikes the ground with her shield and bellows an order.

Each creature of Serel’s choice within 30 feet that can hear her must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be **compelled to focus on her** until the end of their next turn.

- While compelled, a creature has **disadvantage on attack rolls** against targets other than Serel.
- Additionally, the first time a compelled creature attacks a target other than Serel before the effect ends, it **takes 10 radiant damage** as backlash.

Serel regains one expended use of Anchor Howl when she reduces a creature to 0 hit points.

---

### Reactions

**Opportunist.**  
Dame Serel has advantage on opportunity attacks.
