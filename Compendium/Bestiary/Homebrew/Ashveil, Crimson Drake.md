## 🐲 **Ashveil, Crimson Drake**

_Large dragon, Unaligned_

**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor, Skirmisher agility)  
**Hit Points** 108 (16d10+16)  
**Speed** 40 ft., fly 65 ft.

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
| ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- | ------- |
| 22 (+6) | 22 (+6) | 18 (+6) | 10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 10 (+0) |

**Saving Throws** Dex +9, Con +6, Wis +6  
**Damage Resistances** Fire  
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16  
**Languages** —  
**Challenge** 5 (1,950 XP)

---

### **Drake Bond.**

Ashveil shares Dame Serel’s saving throws while she is mounted.

### **Winged Agility.**

While flying, Ashveil can take the Dash, Disengage, or Dodge action as a bonus action.

---

### **Multiattack.**

Ashveil makes two attacks: one with its bite and one with its tail.

### **Bite.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.

_Hit:_ 13 (2d8+6) piercing damage.

### **Tail Swipe.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.

_Hit:_ 13 (2d8+6) bludgeoning damage.

### **Frightful Screech (Recharge 4-6).**

Ashveil emits a bone-chilling screech. Each enemy within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of their next turn.

### **Wing Gust (Recharge 2 Turns).**

Ashveil beats its wings in a powerful gust. Each creature in a 15-foot line must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be pushed 20 feet away and knocked prone.

---

### **Reactions**

**Swift Reposition.**  
When targeted by an attack or harmful effect, Ashveil can fly up to half its movement without provoking opportunity attacks.

### **Bonus Actions**

**Razor Dive.**  
After flying at least 30 feet in a straight line, Ashveil’s next melee attack deals an additional 7 (2d6) damage.

**Evasive.**  
Ashveil can take the Disengage action as a bonus action.
