### *Evocation Cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 120

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** Instantaneous

**Saving Throw:** —

You cast sorcerous energy at one creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack against the target. On a hit, the target takes `dice: 1d8` damage of a type you choose: Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison, Psychic, or Thunder.

If you roll an 8 on a `dice: d8` for this spell, you can roll another `dice: d8`, and add it to the damage. When you cast this spell, the maximum number of these `dice: d8`s you can add to the spell’s damage equals your spellcasting ability modifier.



**At Higher Levels.** Cantrip Upgrade. The damage increases by `dice: 1d8` when you reach levels 5 (`dice: 2d8`), 11 (`dice: 3d8`), and 17 (`dice: 4d8`).
