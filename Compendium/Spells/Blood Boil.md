### *Necromancy Cantrip (Concentration)*

**Casting Time:** 1 Action

**Range:** 30 ft.

**Components:** V, S

**Duration:** up to 1 Round

**Saving Throw:** CON

You attempt to disturb the blood of one creature you can see within range. The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take `dice: 1d4` necrotic damage and be poisoned until the end of your next turn or the spell ends. While it is poisoned by this spell, it has disadvantage on saving throws to maintain concentration and its speed is reduced by half.

Creatures without blood are immune to this spell.


**At Higher Levels.** At 5th level, the spell deals `dice: 2d4` necrotic damage. At 11th level it deals `dice: 3d4`, and at 17th level it deals `dice: 4d4`.
