### *Illusion Cantrip*

**Casting Time:** 1 action

**Range:** 30 ft./10 ft. sphere

**Components:** S

**Duration:** 1 minute

**Saving Throw:** Dex 11

You silently weave a simple magic to aid in theatrical productions or tricks of misdirection and subtlety. You create one of the following magical effects within range:

You conjure illusory words or images in a 5 foot cube that you can see within range that last for 1 minute. This illusion is invisible, but you can see it as if it were not.
You immediately don or doff a piece of armor or clothing that you touch. If you target a costume, you may don or doff the entire costume so long as you have worn it before.
You cause a rope in a coil or on a pulley or similar arrangement within range to either lower or raise back up, or cause a knot within range to become untied if that knot can be undone by simply pulling on one end.
Choose one creature or object within range. If you cannot see the target, you must instead touch it as part of casting the spell. If the target is or is held by an unwilling creature, the creature can make a Dexterity saving throw to avoid being touched, causing the spell to fail on a successful save. The target becomes illuminated by your choice of bright or dim light for 1 minute, as if by a spotlight. This light fills the target's space but goes no further and moves with the target, remaining in its space at all times.
You create an invisible area of sound projection magic that fills a 10 foot radius sphere centered on a point you can see within range, lasting 1 minute. Creatures within a number of feet equal to 100 times your spellcasting modifier (minimum 100 feet) from the center can hear any sounds generated within the area as loudly and clearly as if they were no further than 5 feet away from the sound.
You fasten an object that you are holding in one hand to another object or creature that you touch it to. The object stays affixed to the target as though it were solidly glued there for 1 minute, or until more than 100 lbs is placed upon the fastening. If the target is or is held by an unwilling creature, the creature can make a Dexterity saving throw to avoid being touched, causing the spell to fail on a successful save. This ability never deals damage directly, even if a weapon is fastened to the target blade-first. However, if a dangerous material such as something burning or corrosive is attached to a target by this spell, that material may damage the target at the end of its turns.
If you cast this spell multiple times, you can have up to three of its non-instantaneous effects active at a time, and you can dismiss such an effect as an action.
