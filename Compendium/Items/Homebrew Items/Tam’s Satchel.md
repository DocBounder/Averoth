### 🎒 Tam’s Satchel

**Common Item (contains narrative hooks and minor trinkets)**  
A worn satchel belonging to Tamley Wren, recovered near the edge of the Wildedge. He carried it while running errands for Madame Bennett, who tasked him with recruiting a distraction for the banquet.

**Contents**:

- A folded note with Bennett's seal, reading: _"Secure the distraction. Discretion above all."_
    
- A crude map of Veyrun with a marked route toward the Temple of Renewal
    
- A child's blue scarf (not Tam's)
    
- A small tin of sugar cubes and wax-sealed messages (used for discreet communication)
    
- A brass key stamped with a temple insignia (likely opens a side door or pantry)
    

> _Tam was never meant to be part of the Calling. But the fog does not ask permission._
