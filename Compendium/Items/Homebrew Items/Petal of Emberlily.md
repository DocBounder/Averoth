### 🔥 **Petal of Emberlily**

_Consumable, uncommon, single use_

A fragile, ember-orange flower petal, carefully pressed between pages of prayer.

- As an action, you may **burn the petal** to activate its magic. When you do:
    - You cast _Sanctuary_ on yourself (no components required).
    - You **regain one expended 1st-level spell slot**.

You must finish a long rest before using another Petal of Emberlily.
