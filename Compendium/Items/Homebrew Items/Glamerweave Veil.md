### 🎭 **Glamerweave Veil**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_

This delicate silk veil shimmers with ever-shifting threads of illusion. When worn, it casts a subtle glamour over the face and voice, often shifting to match the wearer’s mood or intent.

- **Veiled Persona** _(1/day)_:  
    You can cast the _Disguise Self_ spell without expending a spell slot or material components. The illusion always includes a piece of dramatic flair—such as a cascade of roses, a jeweled mask, or unnatural eye color.

---

**Attunement**: Any class.  
**Appearance**: A pale, shimmering veil that clings to the skin like smoke and never wrinkles.
