### ✨ **Elixir of Echoed Power**

_Potion, uncommon, single use_

This murky violet tonic glimmers faintly when shaken.

- After drinking this potion, when you cast your next spell of 1st level or higher, the spell **echoes** once at the **start of your next turn**.
    
- The echoed spell uses the same targets and origin, requires no components, and has **half its normal effect** (e.g., half damage or duration).
    
- The echoed spell does **not** trigger another Wild Magic Surge.
