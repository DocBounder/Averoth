### 🕯️ **Charm of the Velvet Hush**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (no attunement required)_

A tiny crimson brooch shaped like a closed eye, often pinned beneath a high collar or inside a curtain hem. It carries a latent enchantment—triggered only by those who know how to whisper the right name.

- **Sanctum of Silence** _(1/day)_:  
    As an action, you may activate the charm to create a **15-foot-radius bubble of magical silence** centered on yourself. This effect functions as the _Silence_ spell (no sound can be made or heard within the area), and lasts for **10 minutes**, or until dismissed early.
    
- **Veiled Activation**:  
    The charm can only be activated in a location known to you (such as your salon or hideout). Attempting to use it elsewhere fails without effect.
    

---

**No Attunement Required**  
**Appearance**: A velvet-lined pin or ribbon, warm to the touch when secrecy is needed.
