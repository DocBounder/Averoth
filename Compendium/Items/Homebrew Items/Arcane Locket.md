### ✨ **Arcane Locket**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a spellcaster)_

This delicate silver locket holds a single shimmering crystal. At dawn, the crystal clears—ready to hold a spark of magic once again.

- **Spellbound Reserve**:  
    Once per long rest, you may **store one 1st-level spell** you know or have prepared inside the locket during a short or long rest.  
    Later, you may cast the stored spell **once without using a spell slot** or material components.  
    
- **Glimmering Tell**:  
    While charged, the locket glows faintly with arcane runes in the caster’s language.
    

---

**Attunement**: Required (spellcaster only)  
**Appearance**: A silver heart or tear-shaped pendant with a shifting magical crystal at its center.
