### 🧠 **Dust of Perfect Memory**

_Wondrous item, uncommon, single use_

This fine crystalline powder can be inhaled or rubbed onto the temples as an action.

- You instantly recall one **specific event or moment** from the **last 24 hours** that you personally experienced, with perfect sensory clarity.
    
- For the next **10 minutes**, you gain **advantage on your next Intelligence (Investigation)** or **Charisma (Deception)** check.
    

**Limitations**:

- You must have been present for the moment recalled.
    
- You may recall only **one event or interaction**, up to **10 minutes** in length.
