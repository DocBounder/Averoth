### 🔮 **Shadowglass Lens**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_

This jagged shard of deep violet crystal is set in a slender eyepiece of bronze wire. When held to the eye, it shifts the world into eerie tones of gray and violet.

- **Pierce the Veil** _(1/day)_:  
    As a bonus action, you may peer through the lens to **see normally in magical darkness** for **1 minute**. During this time, you can also see invisible creatures within 10 feet as faint silhouettes.
    
- **One-Eyed Truth**:  
    While using the lens, you have **advantage on Intelligence (Investigation)** checks to detect hidden doors, traps, or false walls.
    

---

**Attunement**: Any class  
**Appearance**: A strange eyepiece kept on a leather cord or in a case. When looked through, it faintly hums.
