### 🜾 Broken Amulet

**Wondrous Item, uncommon (requires attunement by a marked creature)**  
A crescent-shaped amulet made of pale stone, etched with silver sigils. The markings match one from the dream that began your journey.

- When within 30 feet of the Shrine in the Wildedge, the amulet grows warm and gently pulses.
    
- If inserted into the crescent altar at the shrine, it reveals a hidden sigil beneath the moss and activates a minor divine vision tied to the old gods.
    
- May serve as a key for further relic activation or recognition by Tamar.
    

> _“The past leaves teeth. This once sealed a door… or opened one.”_
