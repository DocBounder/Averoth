### 🛐 **Pendant of the Supplicant**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)_

A humble wooden pendant bound with iron thread and a fragment of sanctified cloth. Its presence is steady, like the weight of memory.

- **Resolve of Faith** _(1/long rest)_:  
    When you fail a saving throw against the **frightened condition**, you may choose to reroll the save.  
    You must use the new result.

---

**Attunement**: Required  
**Appearance**: Worn and weathered, but never rots. Some say it always finds its way back to the faithful.
