### 🌙 **Whispersilk Band**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a sorcerer)_

This shadowy sash wraps around the wrist or neck and always feels faintly warm. Symbols flicker on it at the edge of perception—never the same twice.

---

- **Surge Reservoir** _(1/day)_: When you cast a spell of 1st level or higher, you may choose to roll on the **Wild Magic Surge table** as if you had triggered it. You may decide whether to accept or ignore the result after rolling.
- **Unknown Origin**: Detect Magic reveals this item as both arcane and divine in nature. The _Identify_ spell returns only: “It remembers a name you’ve never spoken.”
---

**Attunement Restriction**: Only a **sorcerer** can unlock the band’s potential. Others feel only warmth and see nothing on its surface.
