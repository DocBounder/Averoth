# Pocket Locket

*Wondrous Item, Rare*. 

*A delicate silver locket hanging from a fine chain, etched with an elegant rune. Despite its unassuming appearance, the locket holds a hint of arcane power, whispering of secrets and hidden potential. Light as a feather and finely crafted, it is both a beautiful accessory and a subtle tool for those who know its secrets.*

A delicate silver locket that hangs from a fine chain. The front is engraved with an elegant rune, hinting at its magical properties. When opened, the locket reveals a pocket-sized, extradimensional space where items can be stored and retrieved with ease.

Properties:

Dimensional Storage: The Pocket Locket can hold up to 20 pounds of material, not exceeding a volume of 1 cubic foot.
Quick Draw: Retrieving an item from the Pocket Locket is quicker than with larger storage items. It requires only a bonus action.
Light as a Feather: The locket weighs only 0.5 lbs., no matter what it contains.
Space Preservation: The contents are held in an extra-dimensional space. If the locket is destroyed, its contents are lost forever in the Astral Plane.
Special Feature:

Hidden Compartment: A small hidden compartment within the locket can hold a single item no larger than a coin. This compartment can only be opened with a specific verbal command or by knowing the rune’s secrets.
Notes:

Warning: Placing the Pocket Locket within another extradimensional space creates a catastrophic rift to the Astral Plane.
