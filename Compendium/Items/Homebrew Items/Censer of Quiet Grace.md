### 🕯 **Censer of Quiet Grace**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a cleric)_

This hand-held censer is forged from aged bronze, its base etched with a dozen names—most of them scratched out by careful hands. When lit, it releases a soft lavender-and-ash smoke that dampens sound and thought alike. The censer feels older than the Church’s current rites, yet still reverent.

---

- **Supplicant’s Drift** _(1/long rest)_:  
    As a bonus action, you may activate the censer for 1 minute. While active:
    
    - Your healing spells restore an additional **+2 hit points per die**.
        
    - All sound within 10 feet is **softened and muffled** (not silence, but heavily dampened).
        
    - Creatures you heal during this time feel a moment of calm and quiet clarity.
        
- **Restful Burn**:  
    If you spend 10 minutes meditating with the censer during a short rest, you may **roll one additional Hit Die** when regaining HP (usable once per day).

---

**Attunement**: Clerics only.  
**Appearance**: Bronze and rope-wrapped iron, faintly warm. Small enough to hang at the belt or from a chain.
