### 🌫️ **Smokeburst Pearl**

_Wondrous item, uncommon, single use_

A smooth, dark pearl that shatters on impact.

- As an action, you may throw the pearl at a point within **30 feet**. The area within a **10-foot radius** becomes **heavily obscured** by clinging smoke for **1 minute**.
    
- While within the smoke, creatures gain **advantage on Dexterity (Stealth)** checks, and magical detection or scrying **automatically fails** inside the cloud.
    
- A strong wind disperses the smoke early.
    

---

> _“What vanishes in the smoke returns in silence.”_
