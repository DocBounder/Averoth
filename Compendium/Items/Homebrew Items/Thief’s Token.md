### 🪙 **Thief’s Token**

_Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement by a rogue)_

This worn silver coin bears an unmarked face on one side and a grinning skull on the other. It always lands on the side you least expect.

- **Veil of Shadows** _(1/day)_:  
    As an action, you may flip the coin to cast _Pass without Trace_ on yourself only. The effect lasts for **1 minute** and does not require concentration.  
    While active, you cannot be tracked by mundane means, and you leave no trace of passage.
    
- **Slippery Luck**:  
    Once per long rest, when you fail a Dexterity (Stealth) check by 2 or less, you may reroll it. You must take the second result.
    

---

**Attunement**: Rogue only  
**Appearance**: A smudged silver coin that never tarnishes, always unnaturally warm in your hand.
